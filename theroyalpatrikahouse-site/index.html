The experience

Every scroll, a small celebration.

Here's what your guests see when they open your invitation, shown on a real one we made for a Bikaner wedding.

i. The opening

A grand entrance, the moment they tap.

The invitation begins sealed. One tap breaks the seal, the envelope opens, rose petals fall and your names appear in gold over an animated scene.

  • Monogram wax seal in your colours
  • Animated couple scene, a palace or a theme you choose
  • Your names and families in rose-gold or gold foil
ii. The functions

Every ceremony gets its own scene.

Mayra, Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Pheras, Reception: each function has its own illustrated scene with the date, time, venue and a "View on map" button.

  • Illustrated or animated scenes for each function
  • One-tap Google Maps for every venue
  • Your rituals described in your own words
iii. The delight

Little moments guests talk about.

Guests scratch a mirror to reveal your dates, play a "who is more likely to" quiz with live results from everyone, and tap swinging lanterns on the countdown.

  • Scratch-to-reveal wedding dates
  • Guest quiz with live results
  • Twirling couple and swinging countdown lanterns
iv. The details

Everything a guest needs to know.

What to wear to each function, a gallery of your photos, family and blessings, and call or WhatsApp buttons for RSVPs, all in one place.

  • Attire guide with colours for every function
  • Photo gallery, music and family blessings
  • Beautiful preview image when shared on WhatsApp

Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Ask us anything on WhatsApp.

Ask a question
Will it work on every phone?

Yes. It opens in any browser on Android or iPhone, straight from WhatsApp. Guests don't need to download anything.

Can we change details after sending it?

Yes. If a time or venue changes, we update the same link and every guest sees the new details. Signature and Royal include updates until the wedding.

Can we have our own link, like rahulwedsriya.in?

Yes. Signature and Royal include your own domain for one year. With Essential you get a free link, and you can add your own domain for ₹999.

Can the invitation be in Hindi or another language?

Yes. We make it in English, Hindi or your regional language. Royal includes two languages, and any package can add a second one.

Can we choose our own colours and theme?

Of course. Share your outfits, décor or a card you love, and we'll design the palette, foil and scenes to match.

What do you need from us?

The couple's and families' names as you want them printed, every function with its date, time and venue, your favourite photos, a song, and RSVP contact numbers. We send you a simple checklist.

How do we pay?

50% to book your dates, and the remaining 50% after you approve the preview, before the link goes live.

Can you make it faster?

Yes, rush delivery in 48 hours is available for 30% extra, depending on our bookings.
