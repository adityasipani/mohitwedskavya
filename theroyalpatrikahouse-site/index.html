Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Ask a question
Do my guests need to install anything?

No. The link opens in any browser on Android or iPhone, straight from WhatsApp. If they can use WhatsApp, they can open it.

Do I need any design or technical skills?

Not at all. You send us your details on WhatsApp and we do everything else, including the domain and the WhatsApp preview image.

Can we book now and send our details later?

Yes. Pay 50% to hold your dates and send your details whenever you're ready. Your delivery time starts from when we have them.

Can we change details after sending it?

Yes. If a time or venue changes, we update the same link and every guest sees the new details. Signature and Royal include updates until the wedding.

Do we need to buy a domain?

No, we arrange it. Signature and Royal include your own domain, like rahulwedsriya.in, for one year. Essential comes with a free link, and a domain can be added for ₹999.

Can it be in Hindi or another language?

Yes. English, Hindi or your regional language. Royal includes two languages, and any package can add a second one.

Can it follow our family's traditions?

Of course. Tell us your functions and rituals, such as Mayra, Haldi or Anand Karaj, and we name and describe them the way your family does.

Can we add our song and photos?

Yes. Signature and Royal include background music of your choice, and every package has a photo gallery.

What happens after we pay?

We send you a short checklist on WhatsApp. Once we have your details, you get a private preview within the promised days, make your changes, then pay the rest and send the link.

Can you make it faster?

Yes, rush delivery in 48 hours is available for 30% extra, depending on our bookings.
